Every ColdSnap product starts as a sealed, single-serve aluminum pod that sits at room temperature until it's ready to use, no refrigeration required, even for dairy. The pods are heat-treated during manufacturing, similar to pasteurization, keeping the ingredients real and the storage simple. Drop a pod into the machine, place a bowl on the platform, and in about 2 minutes patented technology freezes and churns a fresh, ultra-creamy treat to order.
